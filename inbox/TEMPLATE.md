## How to Use This Template

1. **Change the title** to something descriptive (e.g., "Draft email to manager").
2. **Write the goal** in one or two sentences.
3. **List the files** DSAI should read, create, or edit.
4. **Mark what stays online** — anything requiring internet, logins, or external services.
5. **Add context** if needed — preferences, rules, or examples.
6. **Save and name the file** as `inbox/TASK-YYYY-MM-DD-name.md`.
7. **DSAI will read this file and complete the local work.**
